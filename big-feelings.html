Dirty Pages

Sometimes all you can do is
write about it.
The memory and misery,
limerence and lingering.
Yearning and figuring,
all that was entering.
Remembering and de-centering,
a soil no longer fertile.
There is dirt on these pages,
please excuse my garden.
Forgive me for digging,
I don’t want to harden.

You with her
Me a mess

I miss the boring
The reruns and grocery restocking
Naps and big meals
Games and bad movies

You with her

LoveIsAwful

Are we just the same or different now?

Do you still spend
Sundays at mamas house?
Would you carry on and talk about,
our first and last summers down in the South?
Fried chicken and a late night crowd…
When you hear that song,
does it make you proud?
Do you skip our part
or play it loud?
Did you leave for good,
or just step out?

I stand unchanged but different now.

I am a walking shield, not a sponge.
I do not absorb all that is offered.
I do not stay long enough to soak.

I liked being loved from across the room.
How do I learn to stay at the table?
Poor etiquette was absolutely delicate.
May I please be excused?
It seems I have no appetite for words.

@eaturmood

Old oak tree,
do you remember me?

I’ve been with the willows,
learning how to bend,
wallowing in the wind.

How much does it weigh,
to soften in your shade?

Are there rules or fees
to getting off my knees?
Is standing what happens
when I stop asking?

You stay.
I practice doing the same.
