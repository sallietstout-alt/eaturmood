The Origin

Big Feelings, Great Taste.

Many full moons ago, in New Orleans, I was born into magic and contradiction.

I learned that things carry. Sticky air carried jazz and crickets. Oak trees carried bloodlines and secrets. Tall ceilings housed stories of horror and harmony.

Everything I carried came with me.

Up the Mississippi River for structure, then out West to make something of my own.

Today, it lives here — in a world of big feelings, great taste.

Sallie Stout · Branding
